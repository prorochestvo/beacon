# Rename sourceaudit.SeededSource and add fetcher-kind constants

**Status: open.** Raised 2026-11-02 in the review of plan 261101.0007. Severity P3.

- `sourceaudit.SeededSource` now also carries rows read from a database (`--dsn`); its
  `Origin` is either `file:line` or `rate_sources`. A name such as `AuditSource` would fit.
- The fetcher-kind literals `plain` and `chromedp` are repeated in four places
  (`rateagent.go`, `ratesource.go`, `generator.go`, `dbsource.go`). A
  `domain.FetcherKindPlain` / `FetcherKindChromedp` pair, like `RateSourceKindBID`, would make
  a new kind a compile-time change.
