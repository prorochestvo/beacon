# Jusan sources fail on an expired TLS certificate

**Status: open.** Raised 2026-11-02 during Task 1 of plan 261101.0007. Severity P2.

The prerequisite audit on hive-warden-01 reported `FETCH_ERROR` for the Jusan BID and ASK
sources: the certificate of jusan.kz expired on 2026-06-25. This is a server-side fault, not
tied to warden's address, so production collection of these two sources is very likely
failing as well. The nightly audit will report them as repeated transient failures from its
second night.

The same run timed out on `KZ_NATIONALBANK_BID_USD_KZT` and `KZ_KASE_LAST_CCBN_KZT`; their
reachability from production is unknown.

Worth deciding: check `rate_source_health` in a production snapshot, then deactivate,
re-point or wait for the bank to renew the certificate.
