# Nine rate pairs depend on a single provider

Raised while fixing issue #146 (BCC rules re-anchored on the page's new JSON attribute).

Of the twelve pairs `bcc.kz` quotes, nine have no second source: AED, CAD, CHF, GBP, GOLD,
JPY, SILVER, TRY and UZS. USD, EUR and RUB are also quoted by Halyk, Jusan, QazPost or the
National Bank. So one bank changing its markup took nine pairs out of the dataset entirely
for 21 days, from 2026-08-24 until the fix — the metals visibly, because they are what the
owner subscribes to, the rest silently. `SourceHealthAgent` did report the silence on the
day it started; the gap is one of redundancy, not of alerting.

Worth deciding: add a second Kazakhstani bank covering the same currency list (one source
change then degrades coverage rather than removing a pair), or accept single-provider
coverage and treat the health alert as sufficient. A metals feed quoted in USD would need a
conversion and is a different shape of answer for GOLD and SILVER specifically.

Severity: P3 — a judgement call about coverage, not a defect.
